About

I've worked in games since 2007, from environment art to leading quest art teams, and now art direction. I care about worlds that make sense: places built with logic and story, not just good looks.

Outside the studio I run the DiNusty Empire, a 3D art community built on one idea: we get better together. I also mentor environment artists and make courses through Academia of Talent.
