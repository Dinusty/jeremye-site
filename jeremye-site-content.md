## Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune (/swo-a-pirates-fortune)
- Heading: STAR WARS: OUTLAWS — A PIRATES FORTUNE
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/embed/c27kWaF7umo
- Blurb: In Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, keep exploring the world of Star Wars Outlaws with an all-new story, quests, and areas to discover. The Trailblazer's reputation precedes Kay as she runs into veteran pirate Hondo Ohnaka, who is looking to settle old scores with a ruthless gang of pirates.
- Role: Lead Quest Artist · Massive Entertainment / Ubisoft · Release Date: 15 May, 2025

### My Responsibilities
**Art Team & Development**
- Work with the art team to realize the art direction we are presented from concept to engine.
- Create example visuals as in engine guides to better help the team answer bigger thematic visuals when needed.
- Continued reviewing and meetings with art direction to align our goals.
- Organizing feedback to the team through verbal and red pen feedback.

**Production & Documentation**
- Work with production to estimate accurately a backlog for the creation of entire quest locations.
- Manage asset requests from the art team to be ready for reviewing with leads before being handed to the props team to priorities and deliver based on request specifications.
- Work with production on risk mitigations if we are sensing a future scenario.
- Play testing with my counterpart leads, reacting to the entire experience for the benefit of the quest teams.
- Take on director feedback and triage it with leads and production to make sure we focus our energy on the biggest impact changes requested.

## Star Wars Outlaws (/starwars-outlaws)
- Heading: STAR WARS: OUTLAWS — The galaxy is full of opportunity
- Video: https://www.youtube.com/embed/ymcpwq1ltQc
- Blurb: Experience the first-ever open world Star Wars™ action-adventure game and explore distinct locations across the galaxy, both iconic and new. Risk it all as scoundrel Kay Vess, seeking freedom and the means to start a new life.
- Role: Lead Quest Artist · Massive Entertainment / Ubisoft · Release Date: 21 Nov, 2024

### My Responsibilities
**Art Team & Development**
- Work with the art team to realize the art direction we are presented from concept to engine.
- Create example visuals as in engine guides to better help the team answer bigger thematic visuals when needed.
- Continued reviewing and meetings with art direction to align our goals.
- Organising feedback to the team through verbal and red pen feedback.
- Play testing with my counterpart leads, reacting to the entire experience for the benefit of the quest cell.
- Help identify areas an artist can improve on individually to be a stronger artist as well as a more well rounded developer.

**Production & Documentation**
- Develop and utilize pipelines for large scale production for quest creation, cinematics, and realizations with all the teams who are involved in the process.
- Build onboarding and other forms of documentation to help from an art production perspective.
- Work with production to estimate accurately a backlog for the creation of entire quest locations.
- Manage asset requests from the art team to be ready for reviewing with leads before being handed to the props team to priorities and deliver based on request specifications.
- Work with production on risk mitigations if we are sensing a future scenario.
- Play testing with my counterpart leads, reacting to the entire experience for the benefit of the quest teams.
- Take on director feedback and triage it with leads and production to make sure we focus our energy on the biggest impact changes requested.

**Lead Studio & Co-Development**
- Support co-developers with weekly meetings and act as a direct line to the main studio for any and all topics.
- Identify blockers with the art teams and or leads to come to solutions by exercises and or brainstorm meetings.
- Review and red pen cut scenes and dialog sequences. Feedback is presented and delivered to the relevant teams.

## Blog (/blog-news)
### Launch of my personal website / HUB — News · Mar 6, 2025 · Jeremy Estrellado
Images: MainBanner.png, 67bc3133-289c-4b24-afff-5b24046278fc/Communities.png

Hey everyone! I'm excited to announce the launch of my new website, Jeremye.dev. This platform brings together all my projects and activities in one place, making it easier for you to follow along and engage with my work.

**What to find here:**
- **Game Development & Art Direction:** I share updates on my latest projects, insights into my creative process, and lessons learned from my experience in the industry since joining in 2007. Art Direction is something new that I'm diving into professionally and I would like to take you along for the journey.
- **World-Building & Design Thinking:** Explore articles and resources focused on creating immersive, logically driven worlds for interactive experiences. While short right now I plan to explore this type of content and this is where I would like to post it.
- **Educational Initiatives:** Learn about my collaborations with various communities, including:
  - **The DiNusty Empire:** A 3D artist community dedicated to fostering continuous learning and improvement among artists. I created this quite a while back now. This is my main source of happiness when it comes to growing not just me but others. We become better together.
  - **Projects:** Discover other educational resources and programs I'm developing to support aspiring artists and developers. To touch on lightly right now, Academia of Talent with the owner of Lighting bot, Recently posting a Intro to Unreal Engine course.
  - **Collaborations:** Browse showcases of my past and ongoing projects, including new ventures and partnerships.

**Stay Updated:** For those interested in receiving occasional updates, I've introduced a newsletter you can subscribe to. Rest assured, I won't be posting excessively; updates will be rare but valuable. You can sign up with your email address on the Subscribe section of the website.

I invite you to visit Jeremye.dev and explore the content. Whether you're a fellow developer, artist, educator, or simply curious about my work, I hope you'll find something of interest.

Thank you for your continued support, and I look forward to sharing more with you in the future.
