Work / Star Wars Outlaws

Star Wars Outlaws

The galaxy is full of opportunity

Role
Lead Quest Artist
Studio
Massive Entertainment / Ubisoft
Released
21 Nov 2024

The first-ever open world Star Wars action-adventure game. Explore distinct locations across the galaxy, both iconic and new, as scoundrel Kay Vess, seeking freedom and the means to start a new life.

PC, Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5. On Steam and StarWars.com.

What I did

Art team & development

  • Worked with the art team to realize the art direction from concept to engine.
  • Created in-engine example visuals to guide the team on bigger thematic questions.
  • Held ongoing reviews and meetings with art direction to keep our goals aligned.
  • Organized feedback for the team through verbal and red-pen feedback.
  • Playtested with fellow leads, reacting to the full experience for the quest cell.
  • Helped individual artists find where to grow, as artists and as well-rounded developers.

Production & documentation

  • Built and ran pipelines for large-scale quest creation, cinematics and realizations across teams.
  • Wrote onboarding and documentation from an art production perspective.
  • Worked with production to estimate the backlog for entire quest locations.
  • Managed asset requests from the art team, reviewed with leads before handing to the props team.
  • Planned risk mitigation with production ahead of potential problems.
  • Triaged director feedback with leads and production to focus on the highest-impact changes.

Lead studio & co-development

  • Supported co-developers with weekly meetings, acting as their direct line to the main studio.
  • Identified blockers with art teams and leads, and solved them through exercises and brainstorms.
  • Reviewed and red-penned cutscenes and dialog sequences, delivering feedback to the relevant teams.
