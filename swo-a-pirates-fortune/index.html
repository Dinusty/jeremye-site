A Pirate's Fortune

Star Wars Outlaws story pack

Role
Lead Quest Artist
Studio
Massive Entertainment / Ubisoft
Released
15 May 2025

Keep exploring the world of Star Wars Outlaws with an all-new story, quests and areas to discover. The Trailblazer's reputation precedes Kay as she runs into veteran pirate Hondo Ohnaka, who is looking to settle old scores with a ruthless gang of pirates.

PC, Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5. On Steam and StarWars.com.

What I did

Art team & development

  • Worked with the art team to realize the art direction from concept to engine.
  • Created in-engine example visuals to guide the team on bigger thematic questions.
  • Held ongoing reviews and meetings with art direction to keep our goals aligned.
  • Organized feedback for the team through verbal and red-pen feedback.

Production & documentation

  • Worked with production to estimate the backlog for entire quest locations.
  • Managed asset requests from the art team, reviewed with leads before handing to the props team.
  • Planned risk mitigation with production ahead of potential problems.
  • Playtested with fellow leads, reacting to the full experience for the quest teams.
  • Triaged director feedback with leads and production to focus on the highest-impact changes.
