The Division

Expansion I: Underground

Environment Artist

Massive Entertainment / Ubisoft

Release Date: 28 Jun, 2016

The first expansion for The Division opens up the unexplored tunnels, subways and sewers beneath New York City, where agents take on procedurally generated operations from a new base under the post office.

The Terminal

The Terminal is part of an old rail line that was under the post office. With the post office being brought back online as the Base of Operations during the second wave activation of agents, it was a logical step to use the rail line as a means to get around the city and act quickly on situations as they unfold.

Dragon's Nest

Dragon's Nest is an Incursion for The Division. The Joint Task Force (JTF) are investigating a massing of Cleaner forces in the Port Control Transit Terminal. A squad of Division agents join the investigation and ultimately uncover the Cleaners' new secret weapon: a fire truck mounted with a flamethrower and incendiary mortar launcher, christened the "Dragon." The agents must destroy "Dragon One" before it ever sees the light of day and wreaks havoc upon New York City.

Thanks to Chris Radsby, who assisted and helped me finish out some of the starting areas so I could focus on the rooftop encounter.

PC, PlayStation 4, Xbox One
