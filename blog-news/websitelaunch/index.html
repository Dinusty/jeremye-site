Launch of my personal website / hub

Jeremy Estrellado, 6 March 2025

Hey everyone! I'm excited to announce the launch of my new website, jeremye.dev. It brings together all my projects and activities in one place, making it easier for you to follow along and engage with my work.

What to find here

Game development & art direction
Updates on my latest projects, insights into my creative process, and lessons learned since joining the industry in 2007. Art direction is something new I'm diving into professionally, and I'd like to take you along for the journey.
World-building & design thinking
Articles and resources on creating immersive, logically driven worlds for interactive experiences. It's short for now, but this is where I plan to post that kind of content.
The DiNusty Empire
A 3D artist community dedicated to continuous learning and improvement. I created it quite a while back, and it's my main source of happiness when it comes to growing not just me but others. We become better together.
Education projects
Other resources and programs I'm developing for aspiring artists and developers, including Academia of Talent with the owner of Lighting Bot, which recently posted an Intro to Unreal Engine course.
Collaborations
Showcases of past and ongoing projects, including new ventures and partnerships.

Stay updated

If you'd like occasional updates, you can subscribe to my newsletter. I won't post excessively; updates will be rare but valuable.

Whether you're a fellow developer, artist, educator, or just curious about my work, I hope you'll find something of interest here. Thank you for your support.
